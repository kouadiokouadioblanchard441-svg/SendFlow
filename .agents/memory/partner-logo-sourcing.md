---
name: Partner logo sourcing
description: How to handle third-party payment operator logos when the exact approved asset is not available locally.
---

Do not reuse a local operator logo for a different brand. If the exact official asset cannot be verified, use a clearly labeled brand wordmark or wait for an approved asset from the user.

**Why:** A local image can look plausible while representing a different operator, which is worse than a visibly provisional wordmark in a financial product interface.

**How to apply:** Verify the operator name attached to every asset before adding it to feature cards, routes, compatibility lists, or admin-managed media.
