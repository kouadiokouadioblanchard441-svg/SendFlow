---
name: SendFlow visual contrast
description: The user's visual direction for SendFlow customer-facing pages.
---

For SendFlow, customer-facing pages should not look pale or washed-out. Prefer white surfaces over an all-purple background, with violet used as a clear accent and a slightly raised finish where appropriate.

**Why:** the user asked for a professional financial app with strong readability, then clarified that LinkFlow's purple background was too much and should use white like the Deposit page.

**How to apply:** when styling SendFlow pages, use white cards on a cool neutral background, keep body text and controls high-contrast, and reserve violet for selected controls, focus states, and primary actions. Keep requested artwork fixed and visible outside the opaque working surfaces.
