---
name: SendFlow visual contrast
description: The user's visual direction for SendFlow customer-facing pages.
---

For SendFlow, customer-facing pages should not look pale or washed-out. Prefer white surfaces over an all-purple background, with violet used as a clear accent and a slightly raised finish where appropriate. LinkFlow must remain visually stable and keep its existing page structure.

**Why:** the user asked for a professional financial app with strong readability, clarified that LinkFlow's purple background was too much and should use white like the Deposit page, and repeated that its frame must not move or change structure.

**How to apply:** when styling SendFlow pages, use white cards on a cool neutral background, keep body text and controls high-contrast, and reserve violet for selected controls, focus states, and primary actions. Preserve structure and natural vertical scrolling; avoid motion that repositions UI, prevent horizontal spill, and keep requested artwork fixed.
